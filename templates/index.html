Clinical Variable Glossary

cp: Chest Pain Classification

Categorized into Typical Angina (0), Atypical Angina (1), Non-anginal pain (2), or Asymptomatic (3). Key indicator of cardiac vs non-cardiac etiology.

oldpeak & slope: ST Depression & Kinetics

ST depression > 1.0–2.0 mm relative to baseline, combined with horizontal or downsloping ST segments during peak stress, strongly suggests myocardial ischemia.

ca: Fluoroscopy Vessel Calcification

Visualizes between 0 and 4 major epicardial arteries. Vessels showing contrast interruption indicate arterial narrowing or stenosis.

thal: Thallium Myocardial Perfusion Scintigraphy

Distinguishes viable myocardium with transient reversible ischemia (defect resolving at rest) from permanent infarcted myocardial scar (fixed defect).
